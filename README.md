# Pathfind

**Find your next opportunity. Build your own path.**

A free platform that helps high school students discover internships, research programs, competitions, summer programs, and extracurricular activities.

[**Open Pathfind →**](https://getpathfind.com)

[![Watch the demo](media/preview.jpg)](media/pathfind-launch.mp4)

</div>

---

## What you can do

- **Explore opportunities:** Browse programs, internships, research, competitions, and more.
- **Search and filter:** Narrow results by interests, location, type, and other criteria.
- **Build My Path:** Get a personalized set of suggestions based on your profile and goals.
- **Save opportunities:** Keep track of programs to revisit later.
- **Explore colleges and guides:** Learn about potential academic paths.

> Details can change. Always confirm eligibility, deadlines, and requirements on the program's official website. Pathfind keeps sourced opportunities separate from the project ideas it generates.

## Feedback

Found an inaccurate listing or want to share an opportunity? [Open an issue](../../issues) or visit [getpathfind.com](https://getpathfind.com).
